# BottleZero — Demo Video Script

**Target length:** ~2 minutes (check appchallenge.us for the exact limit)
**How to read this:** The **bold "SAY"** lines are your narration — read them naturally, like you're
explaining it to a friend. The *italic "SHOW"* lines are what should be on screen at that moment.
Swap anything in `[brackets]` for your own words. It's okay to pause and re-record a line until it sounds right.

---

### Scene 1 — Hook (you on camera, ~15s)
*SHOW: You, holding a single-use plastic bottle.*

**SAY:** "Hi, I'm `[your name]`, a `[grade, e.g. 10th grader]` from `[city]`, North Carolina.
Every single minute, the world buys about a million plastic bottles — and most of them are never
recycled. I kept seeing them pile up `[at school / at practice / wherever]`, and I wanted to actually
do something about it. So I built an app called BottleZero."

---

### Scene 2 — The core idea (screen recording, ~15s)
*SHOW: The home screen. Tap the big LOG BOTTLE button — the ring fills, confetti pops.*

**SAY:** "The idea is simple. Every time you refill instead of buying a plastic bottle, you log it
with one tap. BottleZero instantly turns that into your real impact — the plastic you avoided,
the CO₂ you prevented, and the money you saved."

---

### Scene 3 — Dashboard & impact (~15s)
*SHOW: Tap the Dashboard tab. Scroll through the charts and the community counter.*

**SAY:** "The dashboard shows your streaks, your daily goal, and your trends over time. There's even a
Python engine that turns your impact into things you can picture — like miles of driving avoided.
And down here, you can see what the whole community has saved, together."

---

### Scene 4 — The map (~15s)
*SHOW: Tap the Map tab. Let the fountains load. Tap one.*

**SAY:** "One of the biggest reasons people buy bottled water is they don't know where to refill.
So the map finds real water fountains near you — this uses live, open-source map data, not something
I typed in by hand. Refilling is always within reach."

---

### Scene 5 — Social & rewards (~15s)
*SHOW: Friends tab (a challenge + leaderboard), then Rewards showing levels/perks.*

**SAY:** "To keep it motivating, you can challenge your friends and climb a shared leaderboard.
And as you save more, you level up — unlocking real perks, like the log button turning gold.
It makes cutting plastic feel like a game instead of a chore."

---

### Scene 6 — It's a real app (~10s)
*SHOW: Tap Share (the generated impact card appears), then the app icon on a phone home screen.*

**SAY:** "Your impact becomes a card you can share anywhere. And BottleZero isn't just a website —
it installs on any phone, works offline, and I built native Android and iOS versions, all from a
single codebase."

---

### Scene 7 — The tech & your story (you on camera, ~20s)
*SHOW: You, back on camera. (Optional: a quick flash of your code or the GitHub repo.)*

**SAY:** "I built this with React, Python, and a cloud database with real user accounts. The hardest
part was a bug where my data silently stopped saving after a thousand entries — it taught me to stop
guessing, reproduce the problem, and actually prove my fix. `[Add one honest sentence: what you
learned, or how it changed your own habits — e.g. 'Now I actually refill instead of buying, and so
does my family.']` Thanks for watching BottleZero."

## Recording tips
- **Screen-record on the phone**, not a webcam pointed at a screen. (iPhone: Control Center → Screen
  Recording. Android: Quick Settings → Screen record.) It looks ten times more professional.
- **Record audio in a quiet room.** Clear narration matters more than fancy editing. Re-do any
  mumbled line.
- **Log a few bottles before you record** so the charts and stats aren't empty.
- **Show the REAL app doing REAL things.** Judges can tell, and it's your biggest advantage — most
  entries can't demo a working, deployed app with a live database.
- **Practice once or twice out loud** before the real take. You'll sound way more natural.
- Keep it **under the time limit**, and end before you ramble. Short and confident beats long.
